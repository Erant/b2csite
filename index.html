b2crunner turns a front-and-back reference sheet of a person into a full-body 3D Gaussian splat (a COLMAP dataset, a PLY and a glTF subject file) in one unattended run on a single GPU. Every figure below is a raw output, straight from the pipeline.

How it works

  1. 01

    Read the sheet

    Split the front and back views, reconstruct the body and its pose, and build a small splat of the photographed face.

  2. 02

    Draw the orbit

    Render outline-and-skeleton views of that body all the way around the subject, with the face composited in, as a control video.

  3. 03

    Paint the views

    A video diffusion model (Wan VACE) paints the orbit in two passes. A splat trained between them gives the second pass a helical re-render to follow.

  4. 04

    Train the splat

    Upscale the frames (SeedVR2), train the final Gaussian splat on them, and export COLMAP, PLY and a glTF subject file.
